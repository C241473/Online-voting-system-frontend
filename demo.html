How easy is it to embed a Poll?

Anonymous Polls

Use case

An anonymous poll is perfect for finding out the majority opinion of a large group. This is the most common type of poll on our platform. It should be used when you post a poll on social media, message boards, or other websites.

Default settings

  • ✔️ Strict check for double voting (via IP Address & additional security measures)
  • ✔️ Participants' names are not requested.
  • ✔️ VPN users are not allowed to vote.
  • ✔️ Nobody is allowed to edit their vote.

Group Polls

Use case

A group poll is the right choice when an individual's opinion and name is important. Participants must enter their name when voting. Group polls are best when you want to make a decision about something in a small group of friends, at school, or at the office.

Default settings

  • ✔️ Low check for double voting (via browser session)
  • ✔️ Participants' names are required.
  • ✔️ VPN users are allowed to vote.
  • ✔️ While poll Adminscan change all votes, each Voter can edit their own vote.

Meeting Polls

Use case

A meeting poll is similar to a group poll, except that the options consist of date & time. Meeting polls are very versatile and should be used whenever you want your participants to vote on availabilities. From group events to joint scheduling with friends, colleagues or business partners, meeting polls can be useful everywhere

Default settings

  • ✔️ Poll options are dates & times.
  • ✔️ Participants' names are required.
  • ✔️ VPN users are allowed to vote.
  • ✔️ While pollAdmins can change all votes, each Voter can edit their own vote.
