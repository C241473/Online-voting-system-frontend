Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VoteAssist collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our platform. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to safeguarding your data with transparency and integrity.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email, and role (voter, poll creator, admin).
  • Poll Data: Your votes, poll participation status, and poll creation data.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information for analytics and security.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to operate and improve the functionality of our voting platform. We use your data to:

  • Authenticate users and assign roles
  • Record and validate votes securely
  • Generate poll results and admin reports
  • Send system alerts or updates (if enabled)

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on encrypted servers located in secure data centers. We use industry-standard protocols including HTTPS, hashed passwords, and vote encryption to prevent unauthorized access or breaches.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies to maintain session integrity and remember your login. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used. You can control cookie usage in your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

VoteAssist may use third-party services for infrastructure (e.g., cloud hosting) or email notifications. These services are contractually obligated to protect your data and are GDPR-compliant.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data and voting records as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can do this through your account settings or by contacting us at privacy@votesphere.com.

9. Legal Compliance

We comply with international data protection regulations including GDPR, and we will disclose information only when required by law or with your explicit consent.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact our support team at contact@VoteSphere.com or join our community chat.
